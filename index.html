What I believe

Earned through 18 years, not borrowed from a framework

on quality
Quality doesn't slip under delivery pressure. It slips when there's no structure to protect it. Build the structure before the pressure arrives.
on safety
Engineers grow when they feel safe to take risks, not when they're told to. Psychological safety is an engineering output — it shows up in how early risks get raised and whether people speak up when something is wrong.
on translation
The best technical decisions happen when engineers and business stakeholders speak the same language. That translation is a leadership job. It works in both directions.
on legacy
A team's capability is the most durable thing you can build. Programmes end. Platforms get replaced. The engineers you've developed carry what they learned into every team after you.
on AI
AI changes how software is built, not why engineering leadership matters. Adoption without dependency, speed without losing craft — these are leadership questions, not technology ones.
